# TPC-DS provenance and release notice

The benchmark artifacts in this directory were produced for academic research
from data generated locally with TPC-DS Tools 4.0.0.

- Official source: <https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/current_specifications5.asp>
- TPC End User License Agreement 2.2:
  <https://www.tpc.org/TPC_Documents_Current_Versions/txt/EULA_v2.2.0.txt>
- TPC Fair Use policies:
  <https://www.tpc.org/tpc_documents_current_versions/current_specifications5.asp>

The official tools archive, extracted source tree, compiled programs, and full
generated SF=1 .dat files are not distributed by this repository. CoRE's
committed artifacts are transformed subsets: they normalize generated rows,
select tables, columns, or rows, add project-authored profiles and constraints,
and arrange the result into paired existing, incoming, and expected states.

CoRE does not claim that its experiments are official or independently audited
TPC Benchmark Results. Any reported measurements are academic research results
and are not comparable to official TPC results.

Council. Before publicly redistributing the transformed data artifacts, the
publisher should confirm that the intended distribution complies with the
current TPC terms or obtain written permission from TPC.
